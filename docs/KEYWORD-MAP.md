# Keyword and page map

**Site:** Ace Bathroom Renovations Brisbane  
**Market:** Greater Brisbane, Queensland, Australia  
**Language:** `en-AU`  
**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

This map assigns **one primary topic to one canonical URL** so pages do not compete with each other. It is a targeting plan, not demand-volume evidence: no Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console data has been used. Validate demand and query wording in Search Console after launch, then adjust titles, internal links and body copy before creating any new page.

## Rules

1. **One primary target per URL.** A page may support several close variants, but it should not try to own another page's primary term.
2. **Services own service intent; locations own suburb intent.** A location page targets `bathroom renovations [suburb]`, not the full service keyword set. Service pages link to relevant locations and locations link back to services.
3. **Commercial pages come first.** Homepage, service pages, locations and quote path carry the call/quote CTAs. Guides support them rather than competing.
4. **No auto-generated combinations.** Do not create `[service] + [suburb]` pages unless there is genuinely unique local content, demand evidence and a maintenance plan.
5. **Excluded terms stay excluded.** See the exclusion register at the end.
6. **Brand terms are secondary.** The business is new; brand search is a small share of demand. Brand terms are still used in titles where they fit within the 60-character limit.

## Cannibalisation controls

| Risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage vs full bathroom service page | Homepage targets the broad head term and links to the service page with descriptive anchor text. The service page owns the detailed `full bathroom renovation` intent. |
| Waterproofing service vs quality page | Service page owns commercial `bathroom waterproofing`; quality page owns `AS 3740` process/quality intent. |
| Cost guide vs calculator vs quote | Cost guide owns research and ranges; calculator owns interactive estimating; quote page owns conversion. Each links to the next step. |
| Service page vs suburb page | Service pages do not target `[service] + [suburb]` in titles. Location pages target the suburb term and link to the relevant service. |
| Blog vs service pages | Guides answer questions and link to the service page for the commercial decision. They do not repeat full service scope or pricing tables. |
| QBCC guide vs About | About owns brand/licence verification; the guide owns the general `how to check a licence` query. |
| Reviews page vs service pages | No review schema or star ratings exist. The reviews page owns the referee policy; service pages carry trust points without review claims. |

## Exclusion register

These terms are deliberately **not** targeted. If they appear in imported data, Search Console or a future keyword tool, do not create pages for them.

**Competitor brands**

- Any named bathroom renovator, builder or franchise brand, including competitors referenced during research.
- Do not use competitor names in titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text or schema.

**Other geographic markets**

- Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Toowoomba, Ipswich, Logan, Redcliffe, Caboolture, Newcastle, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart, Darwin, Canberra.
- Any overseas location: United Kingdom, United States, Utah, New Zealand, Canada.
- Bayside, bayside Brisbane, and other ambiguous area labels not in the service-area data.
- Suburbs outside the 35 km service radius.

**Junk, job-board and classified intent**

- `bathroom renovation jobs`, `bathroom renovator jobs`, `apprenticeship`, `labourer`, `careers`.
- `gumtree`, `zillow`, `realestate.com.au`, `airtasker`, `hipages`, `oneflare`, `bark`, `serviceseeking`.
- `free bathroom`, `cheap bathroom renovation under $1000`, `DIY bathroom renovation`, `bathroom renovation course`.
- `expo`, `showroom`, `warehouse sale`, `clearance`, `second hand`, `used`.
- `bathroom renovation software`, `3D bathroom design app`, `bathroom renovation simulator`.
- Misspellings and spam variants that are not genuine local search behaviour.

**Out-of-scope services**

- Roofing, landscaping, electrical-only, plumbing-only, painting-only, pool construction, new home builds, knockdown rebuilds, commercial kitchen fitouts.
- NDIS work is mentioned only in the accessible bathroom context; do not claim to be an NDIS provider or registered provider.

## Measurement and maintenance

After launch:

1. Add the domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit `sitemap.xml`.
2. After 28 days, export queries by page. Check that each primary target lands on its intended URL.
3. If a supporting page outranks its intended owner, strengthen the owner's internal links and title rather than creating another page.
4. If a genuine new query cluster appears, add it to an existing page first. Create a new URL only when there is distinct intent, unique evidence and a maintenance owner.
5. Review cost ranges and regulatory references quarterly. Update `docs/LOCAL-SEO-COMPLIANCE.md` and this map when pages change.
6. Record any new target here before publishing the page. `npm run check` will still enforce links, schema, word counts and disclosure rules, but it cannot detect keyword cannibalisation.
